## B. Cross-file conflicts and the rule the SOP should use

1. **Colon vs quotes for dialogue** (02, 05, 07, 08 F1).
   - The conflict is real and inside Google's own docs (#16).
   - **Rule:** use the colon form `<visible descriptor> dice con acento rioplatense, <delivery>: <line>`, plus a caption `negativePrompt`.
   - Fallback: if a take comes out silent or mumbled, retry with quotes at the same seed.
   - Note 08's pre-flight check "No `"` around dialogue" stays, but as a default, not a hard ban.
2. **Extension length** (02, 04 used the Gemini API figure of 148 s).
   - On Vertex, plan for **≤ about 37 s per extension chain**, 720p output, no image anchor (#10).
   - Treat extension as a tool for 1–2 hops only.
   - The default for anything longer is **re-anchored i2v clips cut together**.
3. **Designed-voice persistence** (03, 06 said 7 days).
   - That figure is Gemini-API-only. On Agent Platform, persistence is unstated (#20).
   - The voice bible should store `voice_id` + provider + `expire_time`, and Jarvis should re-check before each job.
4. **Omni audio references** (cookbook "5 audio tracks" vs Vertex "not supported").
   - On Vertex there is **no in-model voice lock in any Google video model** today (#24).
   - Voice lock is post-production: STS, TTS + lip-sync, or an audio-driven avatar.
5. **r2v escalation ID** (#25). Before writing "use Fast r2v" into the SOP, run probe P3. If only `-preview` works, flag it as preview-endpoint risk.
6. **Speed-fit wording** (05): Google loops the video above 1.25×. It does not cap the speed-up (#17).
7. **EU penalty figure** (07): use €15M / 3% (#27).
8. **Platform label rules** (06, 07).
   - **Can be stated:** Meta labels its own gen-AI ad features, with the photorealistic-human label next to "Sponsored"; EU Art. 50 applies from Aug 2, 2026.
   - **Must be marked "reported, verify":** TikTok's Jul 21, 2026 rule and Meta's Jun 1, 2026 third-party detection.

## C. Gaps filled during this pass (pain point → new fact)

- **(1) Voices change between clips.**
  - Confirmed at code level that Veo has no voice or accent field (#13).
  - On Vertex, Google's only voice-lock tools sit in **Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS**:
    - **library voice IDs**, with `accent` metadata;
    - **prompted voices stored in the project**;
    - **replicated voices**, kept 1 year after last use, with consent required and AI-generated audio rejected as a source.
  - ElevenLabs Text to Dialogue (10 voices) and STS limits are verified (#19–#21).
  - Net: the SOP's "lock the voice in post" is the only guaranteed method on Google's stack.
- **(2) Accent turns neutral.**
  - Google publishes no language or accent support for Veo, and its prompting guide never mentions accent (#18). Native Veo audio therefore cannot guarantee rioplatense; it is best-effort.
  - STS **cannot** add the accent: it comes from the source (#21).
  - Google itself steers dialect work to `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` (#19).
  - **Guaranteed-accent route, in order:**
    1. Produce the audio first: an Argentine ElevenLabs voice, a Gemini 3.8 library or prompted voice, or a consenting Argentine voice actor converted with STS.
    2. Then either:
       - lip-sync it onto the Veo clip, or
       - drive an audio-driven avatar (note 08 §1).
- **(3) Avatar drifts on extension.**
  - Vertex extension tops out around 37 s, outputs 720p and takes no image (#10).
  - That confirms note 04's "re-anchor every clip on the master still" as the default.
  - The Google sample app also flags drift from text-only rewriting (commit 27f30b6, note 04), consistent with #12.
- **(5) Who says which line.**
  - Google's current best-practices page now covers **Gemini Omni and Veo together** (#16).
  - The verified Google pattern is a *visible descriptor + "says:" / "replies:"* with a closed audio bed (V-Start).
  - Omni `<IMAGE_REF_n>` binding is verified, but Omni gives no per-character voice on Vertex (#24). In multi-character formats each character's voice is a post-production job.
- **Rewriter risk (1, 2, 5).** Google's sample-app rewriter template has no dialogue examples (#12). If Jarvis or any middleware "enhances" prompts with that kind of template, dialogue, accent and speaker lines can disappear. Jarvis must pass the dialogue verbatim and never run a visual-only rewriter over it.
- **Compliance (Argentina).**
  - No Argentine law was found.
  - Bills before the Chamber of Deputies propose a permanent "Contenido Generado por IA" label for AI content used in advertising and social media, and treat AI-made humans in ads as possible misleading advertising under **Ley 24.240** (not passed). — [HCDN proyecto (search summary)](https://rest.hcdn.gob.ar/web/proyectos/290925/adjuntos/105159); [HCDN trámite (search summary)](https://rest.hcdn.gob.ar/web/tramites-parlamentarios/render/adjunto/688a4c2cd846b.pdf)
  - An IAB AI-transparency framework v2 (Aug 2026) reportedly requires labels for avatars, synthetic voices and digital twins (single search summary).
